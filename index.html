Piku is running

Jump rocks, duck birds, and grab berries. Collect 5 berries for Turbo Eyes: Piku runs faster and zaps birds with his eyes.

  • Space↑W or tap the game to jump. Jump again in mid-air for a double jump.
  • ↓S hold to duck under birds and fall faster.
  • P pause or resume.
  • Collect 5 berries to switch on Turbo Eyes: Piku runs faster and shoots birds from his eyes.
